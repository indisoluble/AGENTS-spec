# 01 — Discovery

Discovery uses requester needs, feedback, observations, and existing knowledge to establish what the product aims to achieve and explore what behavior might serve those aims.

- Start with the [established objectives and rationale](deliverables/objectives-and-rationale.md). O1 and O2 retain their priorities. R1–R4 connect requirements to the reasons they support these outcomes.
- Use [open questions and proposed examples](deliverables/discovery.md) to investigate changes. The coverage matrix indexes proposals by requirement. The [proposal sources](docs/proposal-sources.md) explain their provenance and qualifications.
- Check [Formulation’s baseline](../02-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md) for settled behavior and constraints. Record new questions, distinguishing examples, and evidence with the proposal. Technical feasibility findings from Design and observations from Evaluations can inform that work.

Formulation uses Discovery’s deliverables and requester decisions to express agreed behavior precisely. The requester or an authorized delegate decides product acceptance. Preserve each proposal’s status and source when tracing it to an accepted outcome. See [cross-stage traceability](../docs/traceability.md#tracing-a-requirement-and-its-evaluation).
