# Established objectives and rationale for AGENTS-spec

These objectives come from requester direction or accepted project records. Two have an established priority order. Preserving project knowledge supports both. The rationale map below explains how particular kinds of agent behavior serve those aims.

## Established objectives

| Objective | Intended outcome and qualifications |
| --- | --- |
| **[O1 — Control token consumption, first](#r1)** | Bound the next commitment and keep work proportionate. The agent still needs enough reasoning and evidence for correct, complete, consistent and validated work. The objective sets no token quota and promises no measured savings. |
| **[O2 — Requester awareness and consequential control, second](#r2)** | Keep the requester informed and in control of consequential direction without constant supervision. Leave room for routine decisions within the authority the requester has given. |
| **Knowledge continuity** | Preserve project facts and reasons for maintainers, users, and future agents through reliable canonical documentation. This supports both ordered objectives without making every discussion a permanent record or every method a deliverable. |

Assessing these outcomes takes comparative evidence and requester feedback where relevant. Contract size and message counts alone cannot establish savings, comprehension or adherence. Quantitative thresholds and comprehensive behavioral acceptance criteria have not been independently established here.

The priority order follows established requester direction. No comparative reason for ranking O1 ahead of O2 was recorded.

## Rationale map

R1–R4 identify the reasons behind the desired behaviors so a contributor can refer to a reason precisely.

### R1

**Direct contribution to O1:** proportionate commitments and bounded inquiry control the next commitment while retaining sufficient depth, coherence, and evidence-based reassessment.

### R2

**Direct contribution to O2:** routine autonomy, timely awareness, and prior direction for consequential choices keep the requester in control without constant supervision.

### R3

**Supporting evidence and quality:** distinguish intent from observations, learn from results, validate completion, and preserve engineering guarantees so proportionate work remains correct and complete. These support O1/O2 without establishing savings or adherence.

### R4

**Supporting knowledge continuity:** canonical facts, lasting reasons, affected documentation, and actionable handoffs let maintainers, users, and future agents continue with the relevant evidence and authority state.
