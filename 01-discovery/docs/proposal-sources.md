# Proposal and example sources

The proposal record identifies 30 RCx-Py proposals and reports in-principle acceptance for nine. Of the design alternatives D1–D10, it identifies D2-A and D3-A as selected for planning only. The record does not identify which nine proposals received that status or describe the selected alternatives in enough detail to implement them. Neither decision authorized a redesign. The owner's [behavioral acceptance summary](../../02-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md#behavioral-acceptance-and-editorial-provenance) separately establishes the scope of BR approval.

## Scenario qualification provenance

The [coverage matrix](../deliverables/discovery.md#requirement-to-scenario-coverage) includes all 44 identifiers from the owner-supplied, explicitly unaccepted `AGENTS-spec-BDD-scenarios.md` draft. The other 16 proposed examples are BR1.5–BR1.6, BR2.4, BR3.8–BR3.9, BR4.6–BR4.8, BR5.6, BR6.3, BR7.5, BR8.6–BR8.7, BR9.4–BR9.5, and BR10.4. Each suite cites the requirement and the contract or design detail used to qualify its examples. The [28 design illustrations](../../03-design/docs/design-review.md#assessing-design-changes) serve a separate editorial purpose.

For example, the inquiry and reporting proposals draw on [requester limits (§2.3)](../../04-candidate/pre_AGENTS.md#23-effort-estimates-and-requester-limits), [notification and reassessment (§2.4)](../../04-candidate/pre_AGENTS.md#24-context-gathering-and-bounded-inquiry), and [report timing (§3.7)](../../04-candidate/pre_AGENTS.md#37-reporting). Contract detail qualifies a proposal without establishing its acceptance.

The examples preserve the following distinctions for acceptance review.

- **Permissions and recommendations.** BR3.1 preserves discretion over routine choices. BR3.7 preserves qualified consultation for other unsettled material choices. BR4.4–BR4.5 allow useful communication when an inquiry notice is unnecessary, and BR7.2 preserves the qualified recommendation on routine updates.
- **Disclosure and authority.** BR3.5 keeps reporting a material assumption separate from consulting about unresolved intent or permission and from seeking direction when no safe step remains. BR3.6 treats a consequential choice made without required direction as a failure even after disclosure. BR7.1 keeps initial framing distinct from an approval checkpoint. BR8.4 distinguishes narrow assessment-write permission from mandatory record triggers and optional debt recording.
- **Scope and evidence.** BR1.3 applies coherence at review boundaries and preserves advance recovery disclosure for work that cannot stand alone. BR5.2 permits substitution if the evidence is sufficient and the unavailable check was not specifically required. BR10.2 covers known direct dependents whether linked or read, with the need for an edit determined by the effect of the change.
