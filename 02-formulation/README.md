# 02 — Formulation

Formulation uses [Discovery’s objectives, proposals, and examples](../01-discovery/README.md) and requester decisions to express agreed behavior as precise requirements, applicable constraints, and any expressly accepted defining examples.

- Start with the [product baseline](deliverables/baseline.md) for BR1–BR10, PR1, their qualifications, and the [established constraints](deliverables/baseline.md#established-constraints). Its [acceptance provenance](deliverables/baseline.md#behavioral-acceptance-and-editorial-provenance) identifies the authority and limits of the formulations.
- Consult the [proposal disposition](docs/candidate-disposition.md) to distinguish accepted outcomes from inferred details. Keep [open questions](../01-discovery/deliverables/open-questions.md) and [proposed examples](../01-discovery/deliverables/example-coverage.md) available for review in Discovery. All existing behavioral examples remain proposed.
- When recording an authorized decision, preserve its scope, conditions, exceptions, and source. The requester or an authorized delegate decides acceptance. See the [scope of development decisions](../docs/authority.md#development-decisions).

[Design](../03-design/README.md) uses accepted intent to select mechanisms. [Evaluations](../05-evaluations/README.md) uses it to establish independent expectations for requirements-led checks. Current clause-based cases retain their existing interpretation.
