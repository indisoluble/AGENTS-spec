# Requirement-to-scenario coverage

The matrix indexes 60 proposed examples, 51 Direct and 9 Detail review. Discovery owns the [classification and status of each example](../../01-discovery/deliverables/example-coverage.md#reading-a-proposed-example). Here, a Direct case illustrates the accepted rule at its stated strength. A Detail review case includes a detail whose presence in the example does not make it an accepted requirement. The first two example columns give each case its primary requirement. “Also exercised by” records a cross-requirement interaction without adding an obligation. An indexed relationship alone establishes neither complete specification, product acceptance, behavioral compliance, nor evaluator coverage.
