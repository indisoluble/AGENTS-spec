# Requirement-to-scenario coverage

Discovery owns the proposed examples and their status ([`01-discovery/deliverables/example-coverage.md` § Reading a proposed example](../../01-discovery/deliverables/example-coverage.md#reading-a-proposed-example)). Formulation compares each mapped example with an accepted requirement ([`02-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md`](baseline.md)). Read across a requirement’s row to find its examples, or look up an EX identifier to find every requirement it illustrates.

- **Direct** (51 examples). The proposed outcome illustrates the primary requirement with its qualifications and can be described without prescribing how to achieve it.
- **Detail review** (9 examples). The proposed outcome depends on a particular procedure or interpretation. The note beside each link names that detail. Its inclusion in an example does not establish it as a requirement. This classification can apply even after the requester has answered the underlying question.

Each of the 60 examples appears once in those two columns. “Also exercised by” lists examples whose primary association is elsewhere. For instance, [`01-discovery/deliverables/proposed-examples/bounded-inquiry.md` § EX-BI-003 — Review an inconclusive step before doing more][EX-BI-003] illustrates BR4 and also exercises BR3 and BR6. These relationships record what the proposals illustrate. Acceptance as a defining example takes a separate decision. Completeness, behavioral compliance and evaluation coverage each require separate evidence.
