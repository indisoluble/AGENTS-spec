# 03 — Design

Design selects and explains mechanisms for [Formulation’s accepted requirements and constraints](../02-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md). The selected design binds within its current scope. The delivered `AGENTS.md` supplies the operating instructions within the tool’s actual hierarchy and permissions. Accepting a mechanism as enduring product behavior takes a separate decision. [Stage interfaces](../docs/architecture.md#stage-interfaces) · [Decision and runtime authority](../docs/authority.md)

- **Trace a requirement or clause.** Find its BR, PR or clause number in the [contract clause index](deliverables/contract-design.md#contract-clause-index). Each row leads to the selected mechanism, its Candidate wording and the intent it supports.
- **Understand how an increment works.** Start with the [three interacting layers](deliverables/contract-design.md#three-interacting-layers). The [mechanism specifications](deliverables/contract-design.md#crosscutting-concepts) explain planning and learning, bounded inquiry, requester decisions and reporting, engineering and validation, and knowledge and documentation. The [worked task](deliverables/mechanisms/work-cycle.md#how-the-design-works-in-a-task) shows them in use.
- **Derive or revise a mechanism.** Use [Maintaining Design](docs/maintaining-design.md), the [current decisions and superseded controls](docs/decisions.md#architecture-decisions), and the [method-selection rationale and reference assignments](deliverables/methods-and-references.md).
- **Review delivery.** Use the [five staged-to-installed paths](../docs/architecture.md#staged-sources-and-release-relative-references) and [delivery and portability](deliverables/delivery-and-portability.md) for guide roles, bridge behavior, loading headroom and wording trade-offs.
- **Review an explanation.** Follow the [editorial checks](docs/design-review.md) for its readers. Look up a situation in the [28 non-normative design illustrations](docs/design-illustrations.md).
- **Trace an evaluation effect.** Follow the [clause and case dependencies](../docs/traceability.md#specification-to-evaluation-dependencies). The [evaluation model](../05-evaluations/docs/evaluation-model.md#purpose-and-evaluation-model) explains what the existing cases exercise and what their evidence can establish.
