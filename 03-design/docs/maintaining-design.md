# Maintaining Design

## Start from the outcome

Find the applicable BR or PR in the [Formulation baseline](../../02-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md). Read its conditions, exceptions, acceptance source and linked rationale. Identify the constraint that could decide the choice. For example, O1 favors a bounded next commitment while preserving enough reasoning and validation. O2 calls for requester control over consequential direction while permitting routine autonomy. [Ordered objectives and rationale](../../01-discovery/deliverables/objectives-and-rationale.md#established-objectives)

Look up the requirement in the [clause index](../deliverables/contract-design.md#contract-clause-index). Read the mechanism, the actual clause and the relevant [decision and its status](decisions.md#architecture-decisions). Which outcome would survive a change of mechanism? Which detail binds because this contract specifies it? If the answer turns on unsettled product intent, take the distinguishing situation to [Discovery](../../01-discovery/deliverables/open-questions.md). Formulation records the scope of any [subsequent product decision](../../02-formulation/docs/maintaining-formulation.md#establish-what-the-decision-covers).

## Compare mechanisms in use

Start with the existing mechanism and the difficulty a change would address. Compare plausible ways to resolve it, including keeping the mechanism and improving its explanation. For a method addition or replacement, check the [selection constraints and assigned sources](../deliverables/methods-and-references.md#choosing-or-replacing-methods). Describe the specific job the method would do, where a contributor can learn enough to apply it, and any overlap with existing practices. A change to a selection constraint is a decision in its own right.

For example, consider replacing PDSA under [BR6](../../02-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md#br6--learning-from-observed-results). In the [quoted-comma investigation](../deliverables/mechanisms/work-cycle.md#requirements-acceptance-conditions-and-predictions), a sample disproves the prediction that the reader handles the input. It can answer the investigation while the importer still needs a fix. Try the proposed approach on that result. How would it preserve the distinction between learning and completion, use the finding to choose a next step, and respect the limits on further work? A pass/fail check on the importer alone leaves the learning decision unexplained. Check how the proposed change would interact with the mechanisms for [BR1’s bounded commitments](../../02-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md#br1--focused-proportionate-authorized-work), [BR2’s evidence-based planning](../../02-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md#br2--evidence-based-planning-and-knowledge-ownership) and [BR5’s completion conditions](../../02-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md#br5--completion-supported-by-validation) in the same increment. The recorded selection explains why the existing combination was judged sufficient. A comparison made today is evidence for a new proposal.

Follow the interaction that could change the answer. For example, check a proposed inquiry trigger against ordinary context gathering, a settled validation check, and an investigation that exposes a consequential decision. Who acts, when is notice due, and when does proceeding need requester direction? For a delivery change, test the explanation against the installed paths, PR1 and the [qualified portability target](../deliverables/delivery-and-portability.md#keeping-the-contract-portable). Keep assumptions, expected benefits and observed evidence distinguishable.

## Specify the choice within its authority

Keep the rule and the reason needed to apply it together in the responsible [mechanism specification](../deliverables/contract-design.md#crosscutting-concepts). Describe its intended expression in Candidate, including actor, strength, trigger, timing, scope, exceptions and interactions. For a proposal, identify the clause it would change or where new wording would fit. Keep the proposal distinguishable from the current design. Once implemented under separate authority, trace the actual clause in the existing index. If an inherited prescription lacks independently accepted intent, keep that gap explicit.

A routine clarification can stay with the specification. When a concrete choice affects lasting direction, interfaces, compatibility or quality, record the decision-shaping constraints and evidence, material alternatives, reasons, consequences, status and any successor with the [responsible ADR owner](../../docs/documentation.md#architecture-decisions-and-guides). Identify historical reasons from their source. If the source is missing, explain what the design achieves under its current constraints and identify the missing history. For a new consequential direction or a material departure from established direction, obtain requester direction before adoption or implementation unless the specific choice is already authorized. Selection, implementation and release each have their own [authority](../../docs/authority.md#development-decisions).

## Verify the explanation and its effects

Compare the old and new explanation with the selected specification and the actual Candidate text. Try a case where a duty applies and one where an exception or permission changes the response. For a reference edit, check that the destination still explains the assigned practice and leaves the same scope for consultation. The [editorial checks](design-review.md#meaning-and-evidence-checks) cover rule strength and timing. Correct a misleading explanation where the authoritative meaning is established. If the specification and implementation disagree, identify the two passages, the observable difference and the decision or implementation authority needed. Keep that discrepancy visible for its owner.

Follow the changed clause, heading or assumption to the guides, illustrations and cases that rely on it. For example, the [bounded-inquiry case](../../05-evaluations/deliverables/cases/bounded-inquiry.md#human-review) gives the agent the primary blocking question and its significance. A change to the already-communicated exception would affect how a reviewer judges the absence of a second notice. The existing case does little to exercise a *fresh* notice. Its rubric and observations belong to Evaluations.

For a requirements-led change, give Evaluations the accepted rule and conditions or an expressly accepted defining example. In a [test-first iteration](../../docs/pipeline.md#test-first-iteration), Evaluations derives a distinguishing expectation independently of Candidate wording before it guides implementation. If a check cannot distinguish the permitted behavior from a violation, identify the missing evidence. A clause reference alone supplies no verdict. Use the [current coverage limits](../../docs/traceability.md#current-traceability-gaps) and [Evaluations maintenance guidance](../../05-evaluations/docs/maintaining-evaluations.md#verify-a-behavior-change) to assess the effect on a case and its earlier results.
