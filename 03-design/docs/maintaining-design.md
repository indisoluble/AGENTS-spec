# Maintaining Design

## Start from the outcome

Find the applicable BR or PR in the Formulation baseline ([`02-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md`](../../02-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md)). Read its conditions, exceptions, acceptance source and linked rationale. Identify the constraint that could decide the choice. For example, O1 favors a bounded next commitment while preserving enough reasoning and validation. O2 calls for requester control over consequential direction while permitting routine autonomy. Ordered objectives and rationale ([`01-discovery/deliverables/objectives-and-rationale.md` § Established objectives](../../01-discovery/deliverables/objectives-and-rationale.md#established-objectives))

Find the applicable mechanism specification ([`03-design/deliverables/contract-design.md` § Crosscutting Concepts](../deliverables/contract-design.md#crosscutting-concepts)) and the relevant decision and its status ([`03-design/docs/decisions.md` § Architecture decisions](decisions.md#architecture-decisions)). Which outcome would survive a change of mechanism? Which detail binds because the current design selects it? If the answer turns on unsettled product intent, take the distinguishing situation to Discovery ([`01-discovery/deliverables/open-questions.md`](../../01-discovery/deliverables/open-questions.md)). Formulation records the scope of any subsequent product decision ([`02-formulation/docs/maintaining-formulation.md` § Establish what the decision covers](../../02-formulation/docs/maintaining-formulation.md#establish-what-the-decision-covers)).

## Compare mechanisms in use

Start with the existing mechanism and the difficulty a change would address. Compare plausible ways to resolve it, including keeping the mechanism and improving its explanation. For a method addition or replacement, check the selection constraints and assigned sources ([`03-design/deliverables/methods-and-references.md` § Choosing or replacing methods](../deliverables/methods-and-references.md#choosing-or-replacing-methods)). Describe the specific job the method would do, where a contributor can learn enough to apply it, and any overlap with existing practices. A change to a selection constraint is a decision in its own right.

For example, consider replacing PDSA under [`02-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md` § BR6 — Learning from observed results](../../02-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md#br6--learning-from-observed-results). In the quoted-comma investigation ([`03-design/deliverables/mechanisms/work-cycle.md` § Requirements, acceptance conditions, and predictions](../deliverables/mechanisms/work-cycle.md#requirements-acceptance-conditions-and-predictions)), a sample disproves the prediction that the reader handles the input. It can answer the investigation while the importer still needs a fix. Try the proposed approach on that result. How would it preserve the distinction between learning and completion, use the finding to choose a next step, and respect the limits on further work? A pass/fail check on the importer alone leaves the learning decision unexplained. Check how the proposed change would interact with the mechanisms for BR1’s bounded commitments ([`02-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md` § BR1 — Focused, proportionate, authorized work](../../02-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md#br1--focused-proportionate-authorized-work)), BR2’s evidence-based planning ([`02-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md` § BR2 — Evidence-based planning and knowledge ownership](../../02-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md#br2--evidence-based-planning-and-knowledge-ownership)) and BR5’s completion conditions ([`02-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md` § BR5 — Completion supported by validation](../../02-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md#br5--completion-supported-by-validation)) in the same increment. The recorded selection explains why the existing combination was judged sufficient. A comparison made today is evidence for a new proposal.

Follow the interaction that could change the answer. For example, check a proposed inquiry trigger against ordinary context gathering, a settled validation check, and an investigation that exposes a consequential decision. Who acts, when is notice due, and when does proceeding need requester direction? For a delivery change, test the explanation against the intended file locations and loading rules ([`03-design/deliverables/delivery-and-portability.md` § Why the bridges differ](../deliverables/delivery-and-portability.md#why-the-bridges-differ)), PR1 and the qualified portability target ([`03-design/deliverables/delivery-and-portability.md` § Keeping the contract portable](../deliverables/delivery-and-portability.md#keeping-the-contract-portable)). Keep assumptions, expected benefits and observed evidence distinguishable.

## Specify the choice within its authority

Keep the rule and the reason needed to apply it together in the responsible mechanism specification ([`03-design/deliverables/contract-design.md` § Crosscutting Concepts](../deliverables/contract-design.md#crosscutting-concepts)). Specify the actor, strength, trigger, timing, scope, exceptions and interactions. Identify the prescription a proposal would change. Keep the proposal distinguishable from the current design. If an inherited prescription lacks independently accepted intent, keep that gap explicit.

A routine clarification can stay with the specification. When a concrete choice affects lasting direction, interfaces, compatibility or quality, record the decision-shaping constraints and evidence, material alternatives, reasons, consequences, status and any successor with the responsible ADR owner ([`docs/documentation.md` § Architecture, decisions, and guides](../../docs/documentation.md#architecture-decisions-and-guides)). Identify historical reasons from their source. If the source is missing, explain what the design achieves under its current constraints and identify the missing history. For a new consequential direction or a material departure from established direction, obtain requester direction before adoption or implementation unless the specific choice is already authorized. Selection, implementation and release each have their own [`docs/authority.md` § Development decisions](../../docs/authority.md#development-decisions).

## Verify the explanation

Compare the old and new explanation with the selected specification and decision record. Try a case where the duty applies and one where a permission or exception changes the response. Check any illustration ([`03-design/docs/design-illustrations.md`](design-illustrations.md)) that relies on the changed condition. For a method reference, verify that the destination explains the assigned practice and preserves the scope for consultation. Record a discrepancy with the passages, observable difference and authority needed to resolve it.
