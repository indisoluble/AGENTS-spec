# 03.0 — Design

Design selects and explains mechanisms for Formulation’s accepted requirements and constraints ([`02.0-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md`](../02.0-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md)). The selected design binds within its current scope. The delivered `AGENTS.md` supplies the operating instructions within the tool’s actual hierarchy and permissions. Accepting a mechanism as enduring product behavior takes a separate decision. [`docs/architecture.md` § Stage interfaces](../docs/architecture.md#stage-interfaces) · Decision and runtime authority ([`docs/authority.md`](../docs/authority.md))

- **Understand how an increment works.** Start with [`03.0-design/deliverables/contract-design.md` § Three interacting layers](deliverables/contract-design.md#three-interacting-layers). The mechanism specifications ([`03.0-design/deliverables/contract-design.md` § Crosscutting Concepts](deliverables/contract-design.md#crosscutting-concepts)) explain planning and learning, bounded inquiry, requester decisions and reporting, engineering and validation, and knowledge and documentation. The worked task ([`03.0-design/deliverables/mechanisms/work-cycle.md` § How the design works in a task](deliverables/mechanisms/work-cycle.md#how-the-design-works-in-a-task)) shows them in use.
- **Derive or revise a mechanism.** Use [`03.0-design/docs/maintaining-design.md`](docs/maintaining-design.md), the current decisions and superseded controls ([`03.0-design/docs/decisions.md` § Architecture decisions](docs/decisions.md#architecture-decisions)), and the method-selection rationale and reference assignments ([`03.0-design/deliverables/methods-and-references.md`](deliverables/methods-and-references.md)).
- **Review delivery.** Use [`03.0-design/deliverables/delivery-and-portability.md`](deliverables/delivery-and-portability.md) for guide roles, bridge behavior, loading headroom and wording trade-offs.
- **Review an explanation.** Follow the editorial checks ([`03.0-design/docs/design-review.md`](docs/design-review.md)) for its readers. Look up a situation in the 28 non-normative design illustrations ([`03.0-design/docs/design-illustrations.md`](docs/design-illustrations.md)).
