# 03.1 — Conformance

Conformance defines complementary evaluations of the mechanisms selected in [`03.0-design/README.md`](../03.0-design/README.md), subject to the accepted requirements and constraints in [`02.0-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md`](../02.0-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md). It owns its scenarios, fixtures, assertions, qualitative criteria, executable definitions and coverage. A criterion evaluates the authorized prescription. Refer a missing or ambiguous prescription to Design. Check the accepted baseline for the governing product requirement. If product intent remains unsettled, record the question in Discovery for a requester decision. [`01-discovery/deliverables/open-questions.md`](../01-discovery/deliverables/open-questions.md)

Write the expectation before implementation of the mechanism. Exercise executable checks as early as feasible and identify criteria that require subsequent qualified assessment. A mechanism change can require new or revised checks.

Conformance alone identifies and removes redundant verification. Compare the purpose, semantics, preconditions, observations, assertions, scope and verification capabilities of the applicable Acceptance definitions. Two checks can observe the same execution and establish different properties. Redundancy depends on what each definition can establish across its scope. A particular execution result cannot settle the decision. For each omission, record the mechanism property, the applicable Acceptance criterion and version, and why it suffices. Reconsider that decision when the corresponding Acceptance coverage changes. Acceptance remains responsible for feasible requirements coverage independently. [`02.1-acceptance/deliverables/coverage.md`](../02.1-acceptance/deliverables/coverage.md)

The nine cases provisionally housed here still exercise Candidate clauses. Their original criteria and provenance remain in force. Independent derivation from Design and complementary coverage remain outstanding. [`03.1-conformance/deliverables/coverage.md`](deliverables/coverage.md)

The case specifications own fixture semantics, assertion meanings, qualitative criteria, evidence needed to assess them, and interpretation limits. The specialized scope comparison remains here with its fixture-specific exception. [`03.1-conformance/deliverables/cases/scope-discipline.md` § E18](deliverables/cases/scope-discipline.md#e18)
