# 05 — Evaluation

Evaluation owns the integrated evaluator. It registers cases, selects and binds the Candidate, sets selection and execution order, orchestrates the run, and applies aggregate outcome and exit-status policy. It retains run evidence and provenance, applies the definition owners’ criteria, and consolidates findings and engineering conclusions. Record incomplete or inconclusive verification and route a question to the owner with authority to resolve it. [`05-evaluation/docs/framework-design.md` § Contract injection and fixture inputs](docs/framework-design.md#contract-injection-and-fixture-inputs) · [`05-evaluation/docs/running-and-reviewing.md` § Outcomes and exit codes](docs/running-and-reviewing.md#outcomes-and-exit-codes)

Requirements-derived definitions belong to Acceptance. Conformance provisionally owns the nine current behavioral definitions. The engine supplies reusable infrastructure. In the standalone smoke demonstration a substitute edits a synthetic workspace. The registered smoke case goes through the integrated runner and exercises the selected Candidate. Both check infrastructure. Behavioral conclusions require a behavioral criterion and evidence that exercises it. ## Evaluator requirements

E1–E40 specify what the evaluator must do. For the registered cases, Evaluation owns the integrated obligations to execute, capture, report and retain evidence. The case definitions govern what their assertions and rubrics assess. Under each requirement, **Checks** identifies ways to verify it. A listed live check or human review requires corresponding run evidence before anyone can claim it succeeded.

| Canonical owner | IDs | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Engine](../engine/README.md) | E1, E2, E4, E7, E9, E10, E16, E17, E22–E24, E27–E31 | Smoke grading, reusable workspace, process and evidence guarantees and the functional sandbox. |
| [Conformance definition](../03.1-conformance/deliverables/cases/scope-discipline.md#e18) | E18 | Provisional ownership of the specialized scope-fixture comparison. |
| [Evaluation](deliverables/requirements/) | E3, E5, E6, E8, E11–E15, E19–E21, E25, E26, E32–E40 | Integrated errors, selection, order, execution, evidence, outcomes and pending human review, including named-case execution and functional-check lifecycle. |
