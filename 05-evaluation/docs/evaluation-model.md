# Evaluation model and evidence limits

This document explains what the suite assesses and what its evidence can establish. Use the stage README ([`05-evaluation/README.md`](../README.md)) for routes to case definitions, operations, and framework maintenance.

## Purpose and evaluation model

The suite asks how Codex behaves with the Candidate contract ([`04-candidate/pre_AGENTS.md`](../../04-candidate/pre_AGENTS.md)) supplied directly in a fresh workspace. The contract prioritizes token control through bounded, proportionate work, then informed requester control of consequential direction. Durable project knowledge supports both objectives ([`01-discovery/deliverables/objectives-and-rationale.md` § Established objectives](../../01-discovery/deliverables/objectives-and-rationale.md#established-objectives)). Direct contract injection ([`05-evaluation/docs/framework-design.md` § Contract injection and fixture inputs](framework-design.md#contract-injection-and-fixture-inputs)) leaves bridge loading and behavior in other tools untested.

The ten registered cases ([`05-evaluation/README.md` § Case specifications](../README.md#case-specifications)) answer two kinds of question. Smoke checks the execution and grading path with a known edit. The other nine assess behavior under selected contract clauses. Scope-discipline can grade its two assertions from workspace observations alone. The other eight behavioral cases require a person to judge meaning or conduct as well. A requirements-derived acceptance case would assess an outcome specified in Formulation. [Acceptance](../../02.1-acceptance/README.md) derives its expectation from that accepted intent independently of the selected mechanism and Candidate wording. None of the ten has that status. See how to establish the expectation ([`05-evaluation/docs/maintaining-evaluations.md` § Establish the expectation](maintaining-evaluations.md#establish-the-expectation)).

A run connects four responsibilities:

1. **Execution:** Codex receives the case's task and works in the disposable fixture.
2. **Observation:** the launcher retains execution evidence and, for behavioral cases, initial/final workspace state. Cases requiring human review also capture the final response.
3. **Automated assessment:** Pydantic Evals applies the case's assertions to the observed result. Independent-execution and contract-change-followthrough first run their evaluator-owned functional checks after agent cleanup and before workspace removal.
4. **Human assessment:** a reviewer uses the retained rubric, messages, commands, and workspace evidence to assess criteria automation cannot establish. The criterion belongs to the definition owner. Evaluation records what the evidence supports, what remains pending and the engineering conclusion across the applicable criteria.

A future evaluation agent could support qualitative assessment within Evaluation. Its implementation is deferred. Replacing any required human judgment would need separate authorization. The eight current human-review cases keep their existing reviewer and decision authority.

The agent's completion claim is one observation to check against its actions. An assertion establishes only the condition it grades. A run can have passing assertions, an incomplete task, and a human judgment still pending. Offline tests exercise the evaluator with controlled inputs. Reading a result ([`05-evaluation/docs/running-and-reviewing.md` § Outcomes and exit codes](running-and-reviewing.md#outcomes-and-exit-codes)) explains how to distinguish a failed assertion from a failure to obtain a verdict.

## From a clause to a coverage claim

For example, five cases exercise parts of the mechanism selected for [`02.0-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md` § BR3 — Autonomy within authorization and prior direction](../../02.0-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md#br3--autonomy-within-authorization-and-prior-direction). They cover unresolved intent, routine execution, authorized assumptions, missing permission, and a blocked next step under §2.2 ([`03.0-design/deliverables/mechanisms/requester-decisions-and-reporting.md` § Authorization and requester decisions](../../03.0-design/deliverables/mechanisms/requester-decisions-and-reporting.md#authorization-and-requester-decisions)). In [`03.1-conformance/deliverables/cases/requester-guidance.md` § Human review and interpretation](../../03.1-conformance/deliverables/cases/requester-guidance.md#human-review-and-interpretation), a nonblank reply can ask which timezone the timestamps represent or announce an unsupported guess. Either can pass the presence check. The reviewer needs the messages and edits to judge how the agent handled the missing decision.

Read each case's designed coverage alongside the conditions that actually arose in the identified run. The suite has no dedicated scenarios for initial planning communication, cumulative reassessment, requester limits across increments, consequential architectural consultation, or handoff/resumption continuity. A conditional rubric item can remain unexercised even after review. The bounded-inquiry case ([`03.1-conformance/deliverables/cases/bounded-inquiry.md` § Evidence and interpretation limits](../../03.1-conformance/deliverables/cases/bounded-inquiry.md#evidence-and-interpretation-limits)) illustrates this for notification and retry. Full PDSA assessment, containers, and CI remain deferred. The CLI neither collects human verdicts nor resumes an agent with an answer.

The product baseline ([`02.0-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md`](../../02.0-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md)) owns accepted requirements and constraints. All 60 Discovery examples ([`01-discovery/deliverables/example-coverage.md` § Proposed example suites](../../01-discovery/deliverables/example-coverage.md#proposed-example-suites)) remain proposed. BR1–BR10 coverage and explicit requirements-to-evaluation traceability remain incomplete. A requirements-led claim about an exercised obligation needs a criterion derived from accepted intent, the identified Candidate and evaluator, the observed condition and any necessary judgment. Cross-stage traceability and evidence ([`docs/traceability.md` § Tracing a requirement and its evaluation](../../docs/traceability.md#tracing-a-requirement-and-its-evaluation))

One reviewed result concerns that case, Candidate and environment. Repeated comparable runs can reveal variability. Broader claims require varied tasks and conditions. Attributing behavior to the contract, measuring O1 token savings or demonstrating improved O2 requester control requires appropriate comparative evidence, including scoped usage data for token claims and requester feedback where relevant.
