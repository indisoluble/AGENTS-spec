# Evaluator verification map

Each E identifier links to its canonical normative block. The verification routes preserve the original checks, including obligations requiring a live run, human review or a separate sandbox probe. Routes for a split module identify its successor suites. The [test-purpose ledger](../../docs/migration-test-purposes.tsv) resolves individual methods. The smoke case also exercises E5 fixture handling and its integrated end-to-end test. The original five specification groups covered agent execution, workspace preparation and observation, evidence retention and capture, suite execution and results, and functional checks. The [product baseline](../../02.0-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md) owns product requirements. The requirements describe the evaluator. A run establishes only what its evidence and assessment support.

For the ownership correction, the routes below distinguish direct checks of the integrated obligation from fixture or grader checks and reusable infrastructure checks. Integrated tests use offline substitutes. A live run is an authenticated agent evaluation. Where a case requires human review, use its retained rubric to assess the evidence and record the judgment separately, even when the automated assertions pass. [`05-evaluation/docs/running-and-reviewing.md` § Response presence and human review](running-and-reviewing.md#response-presence-and-human-review)
