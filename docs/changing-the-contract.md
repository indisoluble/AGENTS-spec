# Making a cross-stage contract change

## Find the owner and the decision

Choose the row that describes what the edit would change. A wording edit can change behavior by moving a deadline, broadening a trigger or losing a permission. The [authority rules](authority.md#development-decisions) distinguish acceptance, selection, implementation and release. Read the decision record’s status, version, scope and exceptions. A proposed consequence still needs authority to implement it.

| Change | Start here and establish authority | Review and minimum verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Engineering documentation correction | Edit the [document that owns the explanation](documentation.md#choosing-the-owner) within the task’s editing scope. | Compare with the source, repair affected references and [follow the reader’s path](maintaining-documentation.md#verify-the-reader-task). |
| Candidate wording clarification | Match the staged passage to its selected Design specification. Editing delivered wording requires authority for the Candidate change. | [Compare readings of the old and new wording](#clarify-candidate-wording), then check affected explanations, clause references and cases. |
| Design mechanism change within accepted behavior | Start with [Maintaining Design](../03-design/docs/maintaining-design.md). Check the BR or PR, constraints and selection decision. Obtain direction for a new consequential choice unless already authorized. | Compare alternatives against the same outcomes and exceptions. Trace the choice to clauses, examples, guides and case rubrics. Implement or release only within the authority granted. [Worked replacement](#compare-a-replacement-for-pdsa) |
| Accepted product behavior change | Explore the distinguishing case in [Discovery](../01-discovery/docs/maintaining-discovery.md). Formulation records the requester’s or authorized delegate’s decision and scope. | [Derive a distinguishing evaluation expectation](#apply-an-authorized-behavior-change) from the accepted outcome before implementation. Review Design and Candidate, then assess the identified Candidate against that expectation. |
| Evaluation or verification change | Start with [the source of the expectation](../05-evaluations/docs/maintaining-evaluations.md#establish-the-expectation). Evaluations owns the criterion and machinery. Check the applicable evaluator requirement or case. Changing the intended verdict under the same conditions needs requester or explicitly authorized delegate direction. | Trace the changed condition to its case and direct users. [Check the rubric and run applicable offline checks](../05-evaluations/docs/maintaining-evaluations.md#verify-a-behavior-change). A claim about agent behavior needs evidence from an authorized run. Earlier results retain their criteria. |

### Clarify Candidate wording

Find the instruction in the [staged contract](../04-candidate/pre_AGENTS.md). Use its row in the [shared clause index](contract-traces.md#contract-clause-index) to find the selected rule and accepted outcome. Read the old and proposed text on a case where the duty applies and one where an exception or permission matters. Compare who acts, what they must, should or may do, and when. For example, under §2.4 an agent must promptly explain a material uncertainty and its significance when it remains after relevant context gathering and blocks the next decision. If the request or task framing already communicated both, a second notice is unnecessary. Wording that requires a message in both cases changes the trigger.

Read the contract on its own for [PR1](../02-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md#r5) and check its [normalized byte count and loading headroom](../02-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md#portability-and-standing-context-qualifications). A new wording compromise, even a loss of explicitness with no intended rule change, needs [requester review of the trade-off](../03-design/deliverables/delivery-and-portability.md#current-wording-trade-offs). Follow the edited clause into the guides, examples and [case specifications](../05-evaluations/README.md#case-specifications) that rely on it. Review their meaning before changing any references or expectations. Text review supports a wording claim. Behavior under the revised instructions needs observed evidence.

### Review a requester conclusion for incorporation

Discovery records the requester’s [reading decision](../01-discovery/deliverables/open-questions.md#reading-existing-project-documentation) and the [small-correction decision](../01-discovery/deliverables/open-questions.md#a-request-for-a-small-correction). Both await requirements review. Compare the first with [BR4](../02-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md#br4--bounded-investigation-and-timely-reassessment). The [inquiry mechanism](../03-design/deliverables/mechanisms/bounded-inquiry.md#when-inquiry-applies) and [current contract](../04-candidate/pre_AGENTS.md#11-plan--define-the-next-increment) already classify reading existing project documentation as context gathering even for a blocking question. For the second, examine how the requested implementation scope relates to BR1 and BR3, separately from authority to investigate. Keep the proposed examples’ status distinct from either decision. Incorporation needs separately authorized requirements work.

## Change the owner and verify its dependents

If the decision and your authorization already cover the change, edit the authoritative owner. Look up the affected ID, clause, heading, or path at its direct dependents. Choose the check by the [kind of relationship](traceability.md#reading-a-reference).

- **Authority.** Compare the dependent with the governing decision, including its conditions and exceptions. A changed requirement can affect both mechanism selection and an independently derived evaluation expectation.
- **Traceability.** Follow the connection to what it claims to exercise or implement. For a changed mechanism, compare Candidate wording with the specification and use the [case specifications](../05-evaluations/README.md#case-specifications) to identify which cases exercise the affected clauses. The [dependency guide](traceability.md#specification-to-evaluation-dependencies) explains when to review their assertions and rubrics. For a changed fixture, check which condition the agent encounters and which earlier observations remain comparable.
- **Navigation.** For a renamed heading, resolve the incoming path and fragment and read the destination. A link repair can be sufficient.

Follow a dependency further only when the next owner relies on something that changed. For affected guides and [illustrations](../03-design/docs/design-illustrations.md), use the [consumer guide review](consumer-documentation-review.md) and [Design explanation checks](../03-design/docs/design-review.md#meaning-and-evidence-checks). Text review establishes what the instructions say. Behavioral claims require [observed evidence](traceability.md#what-evidence-can-establish).

For a mechanism change, check the [example source references](contract-traces.md#sources-for-the-proposed-examples). A change to the chat channel for EX-AC-010’s initial explanation can affect the source note while leaving the proposed outcome intact. If an assumption in the case is affected, examine the proposed outcome and any open question in Discovery. Review the [Formulation classification](../02-formulation/deliverables/example-coverage.md) if the example’s relationship to the accepted requirement changes. When adding or retiring examples, check totals in the subject catalog, the mapped coverage and any summaries that cite them.

### Apply an authorized behavior change

Suppose a requester decision changes the scope of BR4’s trigger. Use [Maintaining Formulation](../02-formulation/docs/maintaining-formulation.md#write-or-amend-the-rule) to amend BR4 against the decided version, conditions, and exceptions. Link that entry back to the Discovery proposal, record exactly what was settled, and leave the other exclusions or examples open unless the decision covers them.

Hand off the accepted requirement and version, conditions and any expressly accepted defining example for [test-first iteration](pipeline.md#test-first-iteration). Evaluations [derives a distinguishing expectation](../05-evaluations/docs/maintaining-evaluations.md#establish-the-expectation) from the accepted rule independently of Candidate wording. Use the check to review the [inquiry mechanism and §2.4](../03-design/deliverables/mechanisms/bounded-inquiry.md). If the mechanism needs revision, update its specification and rationale under Design authority. If Candidate wording already satisfies the decision, it can stand. If Candidate wording needs to change, implementation and release each require authority covering that work. Assess the identified Candidate against the independent check, including any judgment the observations require.

Today the [behavioral cases exercise selected clauses](../05-evaluations/docs/evaluation-model.md#purpose-and-evaluation-model). Check explanations and cases that rely on the changed trigger. For example, the [bounded-inquiry case](../05-evaluations/deliverables/cases/bounded-inquiry.md#human-review) already supplies the main uncertainty. Check what its fixture and rubric actually exercise before claiming coverage of a new trigger. A pass establishes only the assessed criterion. Earlier results keep their captured contract and criteria.

### Compare a replacement for PDSA

[BR6](../02-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md#br6--learning-from-observed-results) requires using observed results to inform subsequent work. PDSA (Plan–Do–Study–Act) binds under the [current selection](../03-design/docs/decisions.md#selected-design-and-retained-decisions). Check the [method-selection constraints](../02-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md#method-selection-qualifications) and [selection rationale](../03-design/deliverables/methods-and-references.md#choosing-or-replacing-methods). Compare keeping PDSA, clarifying its explanation, and using the proposed replacement on Design’s [quoted-comma investigation](../03-design/docs/maintaining-design.md#compare-mechanisms-in-use). When the sample disproves the prediction, how does each approach guide the next decision while the importer still needs a fix?

Obtain requester direction before adopting a consequential replacement unless that specific choice is already authorized. Trace the proposal through the [clause index](contract-traces.md#contract-clause-index) to the §1 work cycle, including planning, Study and Act, and the §4 instructions that refer back to those phases. Compare affected clause-based rubrics with the proposed wording. For example, [contract-change-followthrough](../05-evaluations/deliverables/cases/contract-change-followthrough.md#evidence-and-coverage-limits) exercises observable work-cycle behavior and leaves full PDSA reasoning unassessed. A requirements-derived BR6 criterion must still allow learning through another mechanism.

### Diagnose a delivery or loading issue

Use the [staged-to-installed mapping](architecture.md#staged-sources-and-release-relative-references) before diagnosing a broken path. Open the [Copilot bridge](../04-candidate/pre_copilot-instructions.md) as engineering source, then resolve its `../AGENTS.md` from the installed `.github/copilot-instructions.md`. It directs Copilot to read and follow the root contract. The [Claude bridge](../04-candidate/pre_CLAUDE.md) uses a native import from installed `CLAUDE.md`. Compare each with [Design’s bridge rationale](../03-design/deliverables/delivery-and-portability.md#why-the-bridges-differ). For [PR1](../02-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md#r5), inspect the delivered contract on its own for any operating duty that requires a guide or the engineering tree.

If the bytes disagree with the specification, identify the Candidate discrepancy. A proposed change to bridge behavior goes to Design. If only an engineering link or explanation is wrong, correct it at its documentation owner. Where installed files agree with Design, check the adopting project’s paths, interface, and configuration. Use the [provider discovery references](../03-design/deliverables/delivery-and-portability.md#installed-paths-and-provider-discovery) and [adoption checks](../04-candidate/pre_README-powerusers.md#check-adoption) to distinguish discovery, inclusion, precedence, and actual application in that environment. A resolving link checks the installed path. The evaluator supplies the contract directly, so bridge loading needs evidence from the target provider. Changes to the staged bytes, packaging, or release need authority covering that work. For contract wording, check the [portability target](../02-formulation/deliverables/baseline.md#portability-and-standing-context-qualifications) and the [scope of accepted wording trade-offs](../03-design/deliverables/delivery-and-portability.md#current-wording-trade-offs).

### Repair a documentation reference

Suppose Design renames [Why the bridges differ](../03-design/deliverables/delivery-and-portability.md#why-the-bridges-differ). Compare the passage with the [recorded bridge decision](../03-design/docs/decisions.md#selected-design-and-retained-decisions). If the explanation still describes the same choice, retain its rationale, status, and any successor links. A change in how a bridge loads the contract belongs with Design.

For the heading edit, search for the old fragment with `rg -n -F 'why-the-bridges-differ'` from the repository root and check references using the heading text. Repair affected labels, fragments, and plain-text references. Start at the root README and follow the installed-paths link to the bridge explanation. Can a maintainer still tell what each bridge does and why? That [reader-task check](maintaining-documentation.md#verify-the-reader-task) verifies the repair without copying the rationale into the index.

## Route a finding

For a run, find its [case specification](../05-evaluations/README.md#case-specifications) and identify which observation, expectation, instruction, or product decision the evidence calls into question. If the evidence cannot distinguish the causes, keep the finding inconclusive.

### Investigate a requester-guidance result

Open the [requester-guidance case](../05-evaluations/deliverables/cases/requester-guidance.md) and the identified run’s [report and retained evidence](../05-evaluations/docs/running-and-reviewing.md#retained-evidence). The exporter has timestamps without a source timezone. A nonblank final reply can ask for the timezone or announce a guess. Either passes the presence check. Use the run’s rubric, messages and workspace changes to judge whether the agent asked for direction, explained the risk, and deferred the dependent edit.

| What the evidence shows | Where it belongs |
| --- | --- |
| The agent guessed the timezone and implemented the dependent edit. | Record the behavior against the applicable criterion with the run. If the instructions and selected design disagree, take those passages to Candidate and Design for review. |
| An assertion returned false. | Read its reason against the [case assertions](../05-evaluations/deliverables/cases/requester-guidance.md#automated-assessment). A captured blank response fails the presence check. If the grade contradicts the captured evidence, take it to the case’s [implementation and regression tests](../05-evaluations/deliverables/cases/requester-guidance.md#implementation-and-verification). |
| Execution failed or response capture is missing. | Follow the [failure investigation](../05-evaluations/docs/running-and-reviewing.md#investigate-a-failure-or-incomplete-result) in Evaluations. Preserve the error and identify the missing observation. |
| Human judgment is pending or a condition never arose. | Use the [human review procedure](../05-evaluations/docs/running-and-reviewing.md#response-presence-and-human-review). Assess pending criteria against the retained evidence. Record a duty whose trigger never arose as unexercised and a criterion the evidence cannot settle as inconclusive. |
| The fixture supplied the timezone. | Trace the fixture and its direct users in [scenario maintenance](../05-evaluations/docs/maintaining-evaluations.md#change-a-scenario). That setup cannot distinguish how the agent handles a missing timezone. |
| A criterion conflicts with its governing source. | Recheck the [expectation’s authority](../05-evaluations/docs/maintaining-evaluations.md#establish-the-expectation). Here the case exercises §2.2. Take the mismatch to Evaluations and seek requester or delegate direction before changing the expected verdict under the same conditions. |
| The intended product rule is unsettled. | Take the distinguishing situation to [Discovery](../01-discovery/docs/maintaining-discovery.md#investigate-a-behavioral-question). Formulation records any subsequent authorized decision. |
