# Established product requirements for AGENTS-spec

This document is the canonical owner of the established product objectives, PR1, and product constraints transferred from [contract-design.md](../../_internal/docs/contract-design.md). Their meaning and acceptance status are unchanged. The [requirements entry point](../README.md) provides domain navigation; this document defines the transferred items.

[contract-design.md](../../_internal/docs/contract-design.md) continues to own current binding design prescriptions and their semantics, selected mechanisms, rationale, trade-offs, design decisions, significant historical alternatives, evolution, and assessment scenarios. Its [Classification and evidence](../../_internal/docs/contract-design.md#classification-and-evidence) and provisional [RC1–RC10 inventory](../../_internal/docs/contract-design.md#requirement-candidates) remain there. Internal requirements add no runtime duties: the delivered `AGENTS.md` remains the complete normative operating contract for consumers and explicitly isolated evaluations. The staged candidate does not govern development of this repository; [architecture](../../_internal/docs/architecture.md#development-time-and-runtime-authority) owns these authority relationships.

## Status and provenance

Established objectives and product requirements are supported by explicit requester direction or accepted project records beyond the implemented clause alone. Objectives state desired outcomes; PR1 states a verifiable product constraint. Established constraints retain their original strength, exceptions, measurement, and revision conditions; a preferred target is not a hard ceiling. The source [classification and evidence](../../_internal/docs/contract-design.md#classification-and-evidence) distinguishes these from selected design mechanisms, current binding prescriptions, provisional candidates, and historical reasoning.

The ordered objectives were already recorded in the design guide and independently in the [documentation reader guidance](../../_internal/docs/documentation-maintenance.md#reader-paths) and [adoption guide](../../contract/prerelease_README.md). The accepted [requirements-led engineering decision](../../_internal/docs/architecture.md#requirements-led-engineering-and-productinternal-separation) and the request for the restructuring recorded there establish the self-contained product boundary and distinguish intended behavior from implementation. The existing method-selection and portability constraints were retained under the request to preserve those constraints; that does not accept additional agent behavior inferred from them. Detailed behavioral acceptance cannot be reconstructed merely from an “accepted contract” commit title or normative wording, so it remains provisional where no separate evidence is available.

Acceptance of product intent does not accept every currently implemented contract clause or make a selected mechanism an enduring product requirement. Currently prescribed methods retain their present contractual force; future requirements acceptance and authorized redesign are separate work. RC1–RC10 and the earlier RCx-Py proposals gain no acceptance, rejection, or revision from this transfer. The source [decision-status index](../../_internal/docs/contract-design.md#decision-status-and-historical-alternatives) and [historical source and interpretation](../../_internal/docs/contract-design.md#historical-source-and-interpretation) retain the relevant selected decisions, limited acceptance of wording compromises, superseded alternatives, and original repository provenance. The [authority-transition history](../../_internal/docs/architecture.md#temporary-migration-authority) retains the earlier engineering-authority transfer and its verification boundaries.

## Established objectives

| Objective | Intended outcome and qualifications |
| --- | --- |
| **O1 — Control token consumption, first** | Bound the next commitment and keep work proportionate. The current design uses small, meaningful increments and progressive detail. Necessary reasoning, completeness, consistency, and validation remain part of the work; a smaller commitment is not permission for insufficient depth. This is a qualitative objective, with no token quota or guarantee of savings. |
| **O2 — Requester awareness and consequential control, second** | Keep the requester informed and in control of consequential direction without constant supervision. Routine execution can remain autonomous within authorization. The exact consultation, notification, reporting, and limit mechanisms are explained in the [current design](../../_internal/docs/contract-design.md#design-choices); this objective alone does not establish each mechanism's permanent trigger or strength. |
| **Knowledge continuity** | Preserve project facts and reasons for maintainers, users, and future agents through reliable canonical documentation. This supports both ordered objectives without making every discussion a permanent record or every method a deliverable. |

These are intended effects. Textual consistency, file size, message counts, and selected methods do not establish measured token savings, comprehension, or agent adherence. Quantitative thresholds and comprehensive behavioral acceptance criteria have not been independently established here.

## Established product requirement

**PR1 — Self-contained operating contract.** The delivered `AGENTS.md` contract shall contain its complete operative instructions without depending on guides, bridge-specific policy, or internal engineering documents for additional operating duties.

This restates the accepted product boundary in verifiable EARS form, rather than accepting every current clause as a requirement. Bridges may still be needed for a provider to load the contract. Guides may explain it, and adopting-project artifacts supply that project's facts, requirements, build commands, and architecture. None supplies hidden generic operating policy. A standalone reading can check textual completeness; loading and actual agent behavior require their own evidence.

### Method-selection qualifications

These selection criteria remain constraints on future additions or replacements unless explicitly revised. A proposed method change should explain the need it addresses, the accessibility of its required interpretive material, and any overlap or expansion of obligations. Changing a selection criterion is itself a design decision to identify explicitly; these constraints on the contract's evolution add no operating duties to ordinary project tasks.

The design document retains the [selection rationale](../../_internal/docs/contract-design.md#choosing-or-replacing-methods): why PDSA, Simple Design, the focused references, documentation conventions, and explicit collaboration rules were judged sufficient for the agreed design, without additional umbrella frameworks. That selection does not freeze those mechanisms as product requirements.

### Portability and standing-context qualifications

The provider evidence supporting the choice retains its recorded **2026-10-04** check date in the [evidence-date owner](../../contract/prerelease_README-powerusers.md#evidence-dates). It has not been newly verified by this restructuring; applicable limits still depend on the actual provider and environment. The [design explanation](../../_internal/docs/contract-design.md#keeping-the-contract-portable) retains the provider-source links and rationale for the approximate project target; those sources do not establish a universal provider limit or automatically revise project policy.

The entire `AGENTS.md` has a **strongly preferred target of about 24,000 UTF-8 bytes after normalizing line endings to LF**. Replace each CRLF pair and any remaining CR with LF before counting the whole file's UTF-8 bytes; do not otherwise trim or reformat its contents. LF and CRLF copies of the same content therefore have the same count. Normalized UTF-8 bytes remain the project's canonical measurement.

Exceeding the target is exceptional but acceptable when further reduction would materially compromise normative fidelity or human comprehensibility. Before such excess is accepted, the contract receives a deliberate compression review. That review removes genuine redundancy and low-value explanatory detail before it compresses core control semantics, such as authorization, requester limits, cumulative reassessment, consultation, inquiry, and continuation. Even over target, the contract keeps meaningful headroom below applicable provider and project combined instruction-loading limits, such as the Codex default documented in the [design explanation](../../_internal/docs/contract-design.md#keeping-the-contract-portable), so directory instructions and other context can coexist. The project defines no separate provider-independent ceiling; those applicable combined limits bound the acceptable excess.

Check the normalized byte count after edits. Automated counts can help; automated enforcement is not required. Normalization defines this project's measurement; provider compatibility still depends on the actual delivered files, other loaded instructions, and tool configuration. Preserve useful spacing and headings. Meeting the target does not establish clarity, compatibility, equivalent behavior, agent adherence, or token savings. Vendor changes can prompt a review but do not automatically change the project policy.

The size target concerns the standing contract itself, not the reasoning or work allowed for a task. Operational meaning remains self-contained in `AGENTS.md`; necessary operating semantics must not move into a README to satisfy the target, because that would undermine portability even if the resulting file were smaller. Compactness is therefore subordinate to normative fidelity and usable comprehension, while standing instruction context remains a constrained resource.

The design document retains the [current wording trade-offs](../../_internal/docs/contract-design.md#current-wording-trade-offs) and their limited acceptance: the identified compromises authorize no further loss of precision. New trade-offs, including reduced explicitness without an intended rule change, remain design decisions to identify for requester review, with the rationale specified there. Previous acceptance and matching counts establish neither losslessness nor behavioral equivalence for a further reduction.
