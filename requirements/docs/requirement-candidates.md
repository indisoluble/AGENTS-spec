# Provisional requirement candidates for AGENTS-spec

This document is the canonical owner of the provisional RC1–RC10 inventory and its unresolved acceptance questions, transferred from [contract-design.md](contract-design.md#requirement-candidates). [product-requirements.md](product-requirements.md) remains the canonical owner of established objectives, PR1, and product constraints; the [requirements entry point](../README.md) provides domain navigation.

The [contract architecture](architecture.md#current-semantics-vocabulary-and-coverage) owns the shared current-semantics vocabulary and contract-clause coverage index. The remaining detailed current semantics, mechanisms, rationale, trade-offs, decisions, historical alternatives, evolution, and assessment scenarios are specified in [contract-design.md](contract-design.md). These explanations and the [candidate contract](../../contract/prerelease_AGENTS.md) are evidence of current implementation, not independent evidence of accepted enduring requirements. The candidate contract does not govern development of this repository; [architecture](../../docs/architecture.md#development-time-and-runtime-authority) owns the authority relationships.

## Status and provenance

**All RC entries are provisional.** They identify potential enduring outcomes and unresolved acceptance choices, not newly accepted requirements or proposals to change the loaded contract. Current operative detail is specified in the linked design topic sections, with contract links for traceability. Acceptance needs requester direction or an accepted project record settling the relevant outcome, modality, triggers, conditions, exceptions, and scope; retaining a detailed design explanation supplies neither that acceptance nor implementation authority.

The shared [classification and evidence](contract-design.md#classification-and-evidence) explains how established requirements, provisional candidates, current design prescriptions, rationale, and history are distinguished; [established-item provenance](product-requirements.md#status-and-provenance) retains the established requirements' acceptance boundaries. This inventory creates no new operating rules and authorizes no candidate-contract changes. Currently prescribed methods retain their present contractual force; future requirements acceptance and authorized redesign remain separate work.

The classification record retains the distinct statuses of RC1–RC10, the earlier 30 RCx-Py proposals (including in-principle acceptance of nine), and the D1–D10 alternatives (including selection of D2-A/D3-A for planning). Those earlier statuses do not establish acceptance of RC1–RC10, reinterpret current behavior, or authorize design changes. The [decision-status index](contract-design.md#decision-status-and-historical-alternatives) and [historical source and interpretation](contract-design.md#historical-source-and-interpretation) retain the selected decisions, limited acceptance of wording compromises, superseded alternatives, and original repository provenance.
